Idaho

Idaho is a state located in the northwest part of the United States. It is bordered by Washington and Oregon to the West, Nevada and Utah to the South, Montana and Wyoming to the East and the panhandle part of the state is bordered by Canada to the north. Idaho is known worldwide for its potatoes and leans heavily into that fact with multiple potato museums spread throughout the state. It is also known for its mountains and beautiful nature once you get out of the parts of the state where the few big cities are located.
